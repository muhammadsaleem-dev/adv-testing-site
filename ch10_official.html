🏛️ Official Q CLOSING SECURITY QUESTION

Source: CH10_Block_F_Security_Testing_Mock_Exam_Prep.pdf

For FinCon, which security risk deserves the strongest test evidence: invalid login, unauthorized transfer, missing audit trail, or prompt injection in an AI assistant?

Justify using:

  • protected asset
  • impact
  • probability
  • testability
  • residual risk

Suggested Answer:

Unauthorized transfer deserves the strongest test evidence because it directly threatens the core operational integrity and survival of FinCon.

Criteria Justification
Protected Asset Customer funds and the core transactional integrity of the FinCon platform.
Impact Extreme / Critical. Direct financial loss, massive regulatory fines, and permanent destruction of customer trust.
Probability High. Fintech APIs and transactional endpoints are the primary targets for organized financial attackers.
Testability High. Can be rigorously verified through negative workflow tests, API boundary tests, and strict permission isolation checks.
Residual Risk Unacceptable. If left untested or mitigated poorly, a successful exploit destroys the business. (Comparatively, invalid login is easily mitigated by standard rate-limiting, and AI prompt injection is typically isolated from the core transactional engine).
