These are the Official Practice Questions provided by the Professor in the slide deck for Chapter 6.

🏛️ Official Q DAY 7 CLOSING QUESTION

Source: CH06_Block_D_Generic_Test_Process.pdf

If FinCon can only be tested for one more hour before release, which test area would you choose — and how would you justify that decision?

Connect your answer to:

  • risk,
  • requirements,
  • economic value,
  • stakeholder decision needs.
👁️ Model Answer & Rubric Click to reveal Suggested Answer

Suggested Answer:

I would allocate the remaining hour exclusively to the transfer authorization and session validation components.

Criteria Justification
Risk This area has the highest exposure (Probability × Impact). A failure here results in immediate financial loss.
Requirements It directly tests the most critical functional requirement (R2: Valid transfer limits) and security requirement (R3: Session validity).
Economic Value Testing this area prevents existential monetary damage and regulatory fines, maximizing the ROI of the final hour.
Stakeholder Needs Stakeholders need confidence that the core business engine is safe to deploy. Lower-risk UI bugs are consciously deferred.
